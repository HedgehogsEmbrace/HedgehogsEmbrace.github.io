About

Hedgehog’s Embrace is my blog. I created it so that I could have a place to express myself to others. Its name was inspired by the “hedgehog’s dilemma”. When it's cold, hedgehogs struggle to gather together for warmth because of their spikes. The hedgehog’s dilemma compares this phenomenon with human beings and the pain that occurs when we are close to or far apart from others. Hopefully you enjoy reading this blog and can gain something from it.
