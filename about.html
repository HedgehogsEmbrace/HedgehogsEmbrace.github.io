About

Hedgehog’s Embrace is my blog. I created it out of a need for a place to express myself to others. It’s name was inspired by the “Hedgehog’s Dilemma”. It is a metaphor which compares hedgehogs' inability to gather together for warmth in the cold because of their spikes with human beings and the reasons they give themselves for remaining apart from others. An embrace is a rejection of this seperation and an acceptance of the pain that comes with closeness. Hopefully you enjoy reading this blog and can gain something from it.
