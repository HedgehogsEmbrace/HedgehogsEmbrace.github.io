About

Hedgehog’s Embrace is my blog. I created it so that I could have a place to express myself to others. Its name was inspired by the “hedgehog’s dilemma”. The hedgehog's dilemma is a metaphor which compares hedgehogs' inability to gather together for warmth when it’s cold because of their spikes to human beings and the pain that occurs when being close to or far apart from others. An embrace is a rejection of this cold seperation and an acceptance of the pain that comes with closeness. Hopefully you enjoy reading this blog and can gain something from it.
