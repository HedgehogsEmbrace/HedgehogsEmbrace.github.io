About

Hedgehog’s Embrace is my blog. It arose out of a need to express myself without fear of judgement from my peers. It’s name was inspired by the “Hedgehog’s Dilemma”, describing how hedgehogs cannot gather together for warmth despite the cold, because of their spikes; a metaphor for the reasons we give ourselves for remaining apart from the human being around us. Hopefully you enjoy reading this blog and can gain something from it.
